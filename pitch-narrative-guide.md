# Selanet Pitch Narrative Guide

**Total Time: 12–15 min** | 15 Slides | 2-Act Structure

---

## ACT 1 — "We're Ready" (Slides 1–7, ~7 min)

> Goal: By Slide 7 (Traction), the investor believes "this team already built it, and it's already working."

---

### Slide 1. Cover (30s)

> "Selanet.ai — AI Agent Nodes as Web Infrastructure."

- Open with numbers immediately after greeting: "2,700 nodes, 90 countries, 30K transactions per day. Already live."
- **Tone**: Confidence. This is not "building" — it's "running."
- Don't explain. Drop the numbers and move.

> Transition: "Let me start with why this matters right now."

---

### Slide 2. Opportunity (1 min)

> "The AI agent market is exploding."

- Lead with two big numbers: **"$220B+ market by 2034. 1.3 billion AI agents by 2028."** (cite Microsoft)
- Then the twist: "But 90% of the web has no API. Agents need browsers to work on the real web — and that infrastructure doesn't exist."
- **25–40%**: "25 to 40 percent of agent operating cost is web access. Sometimes more than the LLM cost itself."

> Transition: "So why doesn't this work today?"

---

### Slide 3. Problem (1 min)

> "AI agents access the web like bots — and that breaks at scale."

- Three points, one sentence each:
  1. "**They get blocked.** GPTBot, ClaudeBot — already banned on most sites."
  2. "**They can't see behind logins.** The most valuable data is behind authentication."
  3. "**They break.** Automation is fragile. At scale, quality collapses."
- **Emotion**: Get the audience nodding — "yes, this is a real problem."

> Transition: "There's a way to solve all three at once."

---

### Slide 4. Solution (1 min)

> "Selanet is a decentralized web execution network powered by real users' devices and logged-in sessions. Live across 90+ regions."

- Three value props, one sentence each:
  1. "**Authenticated & Decentralized** — Real users' logged-in sessions. A resource no one else can provide."
  2. "**AI Native** — API/SDK ready for any agent framework. Structured data extraction."
  3. "**Verifiable** — Cryptographic proof of execution via zk-TLS."
- **Key emphasis**: "Live Now" — this is production, not a concept.

> Transition: "Let me show you how it works."

---

### Slide 5. How It Works (45s)

> "The architecture is simple."

- Point to the flow: "AI Agent → Selanet Protocol → AI Agent Nodes → Web"
- Three points only:
  1. "Real devices — desktop, mobile, and data center nodes across 90+ regions"
  2. "Full browser — read, click, authenticate, and verify via zk-TLS"
  3. "**Sessions stay on the device.** Credentials encrypted locally, per-action rate limiting."
- Always mention the security point — investors will ask "are you using people's accounts?"

> Transition: "Let me show you real use cases."

---

### Slide 6. Use Cases (1 min 30s) — **Most important slide in ACT 1**

> "Same question, different result."

- **Upflux**: "'Find Korean companies that use Salesforce.' ChatGPT stops at the login wall. Upflux, powered by Selanet, accesses LinkedIn through real sessions and pulls verified leads."
- **LinqAlpha**: "'Analyze trending cosmetics brands on Xiaohongshu.' It's a China-only platform — but with local sessions, it works."
- Closing quote: **"AI can answer questions. Selanet lets agents execute behind login walls."**
- **Tip**: This is where investor eyes should light up. Be concrete and vivid.

> Transition: "Let me prove this isn't just a concept — with numbers."

---

### Slide 7. Traction (1 min 15s) — **ACT 1 Climax**

> "Live Infrastructure. Real Pipeline."

- Top stats quickly: "8 LOIs — $25K MRR projected. 25+ pipeline. 4.2s latency. 99%+ success rate. Less than 1% ban rate."
- **Live Now**: "Browse API production-ready. 2,700 nodes deployed across 90+ regions."
- **Converting Now** — deliver with weight: "2 of 8 LOI clients completing integration now. Contracts in legal review. First invoices scheduled this quarter."
- **Tone shift**: Everything up to here was proof. Slightly shift your voice —

> Transition: "Infrastructure is proven. Now let me show you why we win this market."

---

## ACT 2 — "Now We Scale" (Slides 8–15, ~7 min)

> Goal: Clear growth mechanics, grounded financials, and a closing that makes the investor think "this team + this money = inevitable."

---

### Slide 8. Why We Win (1 min) — **ACT 2 Opener, maintain energy**

> "Others sell tools or access. We build the infrastructure layer AI depends on."

- Walk through the quadrant quickly:
  - Bottom-left: "Playwright — local, doesn't scale"
  - Top-left: "Tavily, Browserbase — centralized, fails at high scale"
  - Bottom-right: "Grass — distributed but not agent-native"
  - **Top-right**: "**Selanet** — the only player that's distributed + high-scale + authenticated sessions"
- Competitor funding: "Browserbase raised $40M Series-B. Browser Use raised $17M Seed. Money is flowing into this market."

> Transition: "Why is this position defensible? Network effects."

---

### Slide 9. Flywheel (45s)

> "AI Agent Node breaks the DePIN growth ceiling."

- Five steps in one breath: "More nodes → richer access → more usage → higher revenue → stronger incentives → back to more nodes"
- **One key line**: "Other node networks attract operators with tokens. We retain them with a personal AI agent they use daily. Churn decouples from token price."

> Transition: "Here's how we monetize."

---

### Slide 10. Business Model (1 min)

> "Browse API — web execution as a service."

- Three numbers: "$8 per 1K requests. ~60% gross margin. $25K projected MRR."
- Pricing Ladder: "Free → PAYG → Subscription → Enterprise. Low barrier, expand on usage."
- Revenue Expansion: "When clients go from pilot to production, volume increases 10-100x."

> Transition: "How we sell it."

---

### Slide 11. GTM (45s)

> "Win long-tail first, then scale up."

- Three segments quickly:
  1. "**Now** — Agent startups. PLG acquisition."
  2. "**Q3** — Vertical AI builders. Enterprise sales."
  3. "**Q4** — Platform partners. Thousands of downstream clients."
- **One sentence**: "Start where the most agent builders are. Become the default infrastructure."

> Transition: "Financial projections."

---

### Slide 12. Financials (1 min)

> "Three-year cumulative: $78M. 47.5x revenue multiple."

- Year 1: "$1.3M — 8 LOI clients converting at ~$3K average MRR, plus new pipeline."
- Year 2–3: "Node network scales to 100K+. Usage grows non-linearly."
- **Benchmark**: "Bright Data hit $300M ARR in the same market. We operate at a fraction of the cost with decentralized infrastructure."
- If investors ask about Y1 math: "8 × $3K × 12 = $288K baseline + pipeline conversion and upsell gets to $1.3M"

> Transition: "Roadmap."

---

### Slide 13. Roadmap (30s)

- Three phases, fast:
  1. "**Foundation** — AI Agent Node launch, LOI conversion, 2,000 active nodes"
  2. "**2027–28** — 100K nodes, 150 countries, dedicated node device"
  3. "**2029–30** — 1M+ nodes. The default web layer for AI agents."
- Don't linger. Show the vision and move.

> Transition: *(brief pause, warmer tone)* "Let me introduce the team that will execute this."

---

### Slide 14. Team (1 min) — **Closing Setup**

> "This is BC Labs."

- **Stephen** (CEO): "Former VC investor. Built the entire pipeline himself. 8 LOIs, 25 POCs — with zero external funding."
- **Spock** (CTO): "Three-time CTO. Five years building scraping systems. Designed and shipped the entire P2P execution layer to production."
- Rest quickly: "8 full-time across Dunamu, Samsung, Probit backgrounds."
- **Backed by**: "SBVA — SoftBank Ventures Asia. And KB Investment."
- **Key tone**: "These numbers were built by this team. This team will scale them."

> Transition: *(eye contact, natural)* "Last slide."

---

### Slide 15. The Ask (45s) — **Closing**

> "$1M Seed at $20M FDV."

- Use of Funds quickly: "60% engineering, 20% ecosystem, 15% BD, 5% legal"
- **Closing line** — slow down, eye contact:

> **"Infrastructure is done. Revenue is next."**

- *(2 seconds of silence)*
- "Thank you. Happy to take questions."

**Two peaks**: Traction (end of ACT 1) and Team→Ask (end of ACT 2).
The middle section (BM/GTM/Financials) is deliberately calm — give investors time to process the numbers.

## Delivery Tips

| Area | Do | Don't |
|------|-----|-------|
| **ACT transition** | After Traction: "Infrastructure is proven." — one sentence pivot | Don't say "next slide" or "and then" |
| **Numbers** | Slow down + pause on key stats ("30K transactions. Per day.") | Don't read every number on the slide |
| **Team placement** | After Roadmap, before Ask = "these people will execute" | Don't read team bios like a resume |
| **Closing** | "Infrastructure is done. Revenue is next." then 2s silence | Don't add extra explanation after the closing line |
| **Use Cases** | Storytelling — "this team came to us and said..." | Don't drift into technical architecture |
| **Confidence** | State facts. Let numbers speak. | Don't hedge ("we think", "we hope", "maybe") |

## Q&A Preparation

| Likely Question | Prepared Answer |
|----------------|-----------------|
| "99% success rate vs other sources saying 89%?" | "99%+ is SLA uptime. 89% is job completion rate — which we're improving to 95%+ with AI Agent Node." |
| "What does 'Backed by SBVA/KB' mean exactly?" | *(Clarify the exact relationship — equity, LOI, partnership, etc.)* |
| "How do you handle user session security?" | "Sessions never leave the device. Credentials encrypted locally. Per-action rate limiting. Human-in-the-loop for sensitive actions." |
| "Why $20M FDV?" | "Benchmarked: Grass $20M, Nodepay $25M at similar stage. Bright Data $300M ARR validates the market." |
| "What if platforms crack down on this?" | "Our traffic IS real user traffic. Platforms can't distinguish it because it genuinely comes from real devices and sessions." |
| "Token play concern?" | "Revenue-first model. B2B API fees are the primary revenue. Token is utility layer for node incentives, not the business model." |
| "Why not just use Bright Data?" | "Bright Data is centralized proxies — no authenticated sessions, no post-login access. We provide what they structurally cannot." |
